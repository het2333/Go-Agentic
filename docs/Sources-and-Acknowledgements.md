## Scope of this disclosure

Go Agentic's course narrative, chapter structure, examples, code, and original visual assets are independently authored for this project. This page records external material actually used to verify facts, explain technical background, design exercises, or check topic coverage. Listing a project, author, or institution identifies a source relationship; it does not imply endorsement, sponsorship, affiliation, or participation in maintaining Go Agentic.

The course explains external material in its own language. Direct quotations are marked and attributed at the point of use. Third-party names, trademarks, and linked material remain subject to their respective rights and terms.

## Software projects and design reference

- [Pi](https://pi.dev/docs/latest): the course checked its official site and Coding Agent documentation for sessions, compaction, extensions, terminal-agent harnesses, tools, and extensibility boundaries. Pi is the continuous practical reference; this repository's offline fixtures are independent teaching implementations and do not reimplement the Pi SDK.
- [DeepSeek Harness](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness): the course checked the official repository, [architecture document](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness/blob/master/docs/architecture.md), and [safety notice](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness/blob/master/SAFETY.md) to compare composition boundaries in a pluginized harness.
- [Hermes Agent](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent): the course checked the official repository, [product documentation](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/), and [memory documentation](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/user-guide/features/memory) to compare long-running runtime services such as persistent memory, scheduling, messaging entry points, MCP, and isolated subtasks.
- Reference PDF: H. Roitman, *智能体 AI 漫游指南：从基础到系统*, v1.1.0, local source filename `agentic-ai-guide-zh-v1.1.0.pdf`. During course design it served as a topic checklist for harnesses, loop engineering, environments, evaluation, MCP, skills, A2A, and multi-agent systems. The release retains none of its continuous prose, figures, tables, or chapter structure.

These three software projects and the reference PDF are external references. They are not co-authors and do not participate in maintaining Go Agentic. Capabilities, installation steps, security status, and version behavior can change; consult their primary sources before adoption.
